# Catalog Exporter

A browser extension for Chrome and Microsoft Edge that exports your Whatnot product listings to a CSV
file.

It reads your listings through Whatnot's official Seller API, using a read-only access token you create
on Whatnot, and saves them as one product CSV, ready to import into another sales channel, an inventory
tool or a spreadsheet. Your token and products stay in your browser: the extension has no server of its
own and does no tracking.

- [Privacy policy](privacy-policy)

Catalog Exporter is an independent tool and is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by
Whatnot Inc.
