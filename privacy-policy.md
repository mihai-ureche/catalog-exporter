# Privacy Policy — Catalog Exporter

_Last updated: November 25, 2026_

Catalog Exporter ("the extension") is a browser extension for Chrome and Microsoft Edge, published by
**[Publisher name]**. It reads a seller's own product listings from Whatnot and saves them as a CSV file
on the seller's computer. This policy explains what data the extension handles, where it goes, and how
to remove it.

**In short:** everything the extension handles stays in your browser. It talks only to Whatnot's
official API, using the token you give it. We, the publisher, never receive your token, your products
or anything else about you.

## What the extension handles

| Data                                                                                                                                                   | Why                                                       | Where it is kept                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **Your Whatnot personal access token**, which you paste in                                                                                             | To read your products through Whatnot's Seller API        | Your browser's extension storage, on your computer |
| **Your product listings**, read from Whatnot: titles, descriptions, prices, stock, SKUs, condition, variants, image links and how each product is sold | To show them in the side panel and build the CSV file     | Your browser's extension storage, on your computer |
| **The data Whatnot returned in the last run**, as received                                                                                             | So you can download it as a JSON file to check it         | Your browser's extension storage, on your computer |
| **Your choices and the progress of a run**: which products you unticked for export, and the status and activity log of the last run                    | So the side panel shows the same state when you reopen it | Your browser's extension storage, on your computer |

The token you create on Whatnot only needs **read** permissions (Inventory and Merchandising). The
extension never changes, creates or deletes anything in your Whatnot account.

The extension does **not** collect your name, email address, payment details, location, browsing
history or the content of the web pages you visit. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or
tracking of any kind.

## Where your data goes

- **To Whatnot only.** The extension sends your token to `api.whatnot.com`, Whatnot's official Seller
  API, to read your products. Whatnot's handling of that request is covered by
  [Whatnot's privacy policy](https://www.whatnot.com/privacy).
- **To your computer.** When you press **Export CSV** or **Download API data**, the file is saved to
  your computer like any other download. What you do with the file afterwards (for example, importing
  it into another system) is up to you.
- **Nowhere else.** The extension has no server of its own. We do not receive, store, sell, rent or
  share your data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising, credit decisions or any purpose
  other than the extension's single purpose described above.

## How long it is kept, and how to delete it

Data stays in your browser until you remove it:

- **Clear** in the side panel deletes the collected products, the last API data, your export
  selection and the run history.
- **Remove token** in the side panel deletes the token from the extension. To stop the token from
  working at all, also delete it on Whatnot's token page (Dashboard → Developer → Tokens).
- **Uninstalling the extension** deletes everything it stored.

## Security

Your token and products are kept in the browser's extension storage, which other websites and other
extensions cannot read. All requests to Whatnot use HTTPS. Anyone who can use your browser profile
can open the extension and see what it has stored, so use it on a computer and browser profile you
trust.

## Chrome Web Store user data policy

The use of information received by the extension adheres to the
[Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Children

The extension is a tool for sellers and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in
your country).
